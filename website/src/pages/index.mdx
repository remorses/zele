## Privacy and your Google data

zele is a **local client**. It asks Google for access to Gmail, Google Calendar and your email address so you can read, search, send and organize mail and manage events from your terminal. All Google data goes **directly between your computer and Google**, and tokens and cached mail stay in `~/.zele/sqlite.db` on your machine. zele never sends your Google data to its own servers, never sells it, and never uses it to train AI models.

Read the [privacy policy](./privacy.mdx) and the [terms of service](./terms.mdx).
